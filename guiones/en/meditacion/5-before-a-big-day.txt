# Arriving
@energy 2
> Calm and close, like someone keeping you company.

[slow][soft]Tomorrow is an important day, and it's fine to feel it in your body.
Find a comfortable position and close your eyes.
[breathe]
Breathe in through your nose and fill your chest little by little.
Exhale slowly, as if blowing out a candle with no hurry.
[breathe]
Where do you feel the nerves right now? [up]
Maybe in your stomach, your hands or your throat.
You don't have to make them disappear.
We're just going to give them some room.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Box breathing
@energy 1
> Very calm; count without rushing.

[slow][soft]Let's breathe in four equal counts.
Breathe in, counting to four: one, two, three, four.
Hold your breath, also for four counts.
Breathe out for four, and wait four before starting again.
[breathe]
It's like drawing a square with your breath, no hard rules.
Repeat it a couple of times, at your own pace.
[breathe]
Notice how your heart begins to slow down.
Your body knows how to calm itself; it just needs a reminder.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Imagining it goes well
@energy 2
> Soft, with a calm smile.

[soft]Now picture tomorrow, but going well.
You see yourself arriving on time, with a steady step and your head up.
[breathe]
You say hello, speak calmly and someone smiles back.
If something doesn't go as planned, you breathe and keep going.
[breathe]
You don't need to know everything to begin.
You carry everything you've learned until today.
And that's more than you think.
Hold that vision for a moment.
Keep it like a **gift** for tomorrow.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Letting go
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper.

[slow][soft]Now let go of the image and come back to the present.
Notice the weight of your body, supported and safe.
[breathe]
Whatever needs to happen tomorrow will happen tomorrow.
Tonight, your only job is to rest.
Can you leave tomorrow for tomorrow? [up]
Soften your forehead, your shoulders and your hands.
[breathe]
Feel your breathing become slow and deep on its own.
There's no need to rush.
Each exhale takes you a little closer to rest.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Returning or sleeping
@energy 2
> Very warm at the end.

[slow]If you're going to sleep, stay like this and let sleep come by itself.
If you're going on with your evening, come back little by little.[/slow]
[breathe]
Move your fingers, stretch your arms and open your eyes with calm.
Remember box breathing: it goes with you wherever you go.
[breathe]
Tomorrow, before you walk in, take four breaths like this.
Your nerves aren't an enemy; they're a sign that you care.
Trust yourself. [down]
Sleep well, and good luck.
[pause 3s]
