# Putting the phone down
@energy 2
> Kind and unhurried, without judging.

[slow][soft]Before we begin, put your phone face down, out of reach.
If it buzzes, let it buzz: for a few minutes, nothing is urgent.
[breathe]
Find a comfortable position and rest your back.
Close your eyes and notice your empty hands.
Do you still feel the urge to check the screen? [up]
[breathe]
That's okay; it's normal.
Just watch the urge, like a car passing by the window.
And let it drift away.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Your eyes
@energy 1
> Very soft, taking care with each word.

[soft]Your eyes spend so many hours looking at bright lights.
Now let them rest behind your eyelids.
[breathe]
Feel the small muscles around your eyes relax.
Your forehead softens, and so do your eyebrows and your jaw.
[breathe]
Imagine a warm, kind darkness, like a summer night.
There are no notifications or messages, only silence.
Your mind can turn down the volume, like a screen at sunset.
Breathe in, breathe out, and let everything dim a little.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Your body
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper, moving through the body with calm.

[slow][soft]Now move your attention through your body, from head to toe.
Notice your neck, which so often leans toward the screen.
Let it return to its place, long and free.
[breathe]
Move down through your shoulders, your arms and your hands.
Feel your fingers at rest, not typing, not scrolling.
[breathe]
Continue through your chest, your belly and your legs.
Your whole body rests, with nothing to answer.
This is also a kind of **connection**: with yourself.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# What matters
@energy 2
> Soft, with a touch of reflection.

[soft]Think of something you saw today without a screen in between.
A face, a street, a square, or a painting on a wall.
[breathe]
What did you feel in that moment? [up]
Maybe it was small, but it was real, and it was yours.
[breathe]
The world outside is still there, waiting for you without hurry.
The news can wait a few more minutes.
Your breath, on the other hand, is here now.
Stay with it a little longer.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Returning
@energy 2
> Come back with calm; a warm tone at the end.

[slow]Begin to gently move your fingers and toes.
Stretch your arms and notice the air in the room.[/slow]
[breathe]
Before you pick up your phone, take one more deep breath.
Decide calmly what to check first, and what can wait.
Here's a kind rule: breathe first, scroll later.
It's a small decision that changes your day.
[breathe]
Open your eyes when you're ready.
You can give yourself this pause every day, even for five minutes.
Your attention is valuable: take care of it. [down]
Thank you for giving yourself this time.
[pause 3s]
