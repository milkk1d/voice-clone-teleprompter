# Getting ready
@energy 2
> A soft voice, like someone preparing something with care.

[slow][soft]Imagine it's a cold afternoon and you're at home.
Outside, rain falls gently and the sky is gray.
[breathe]
In your hands you hold a cup of hot chocolate.
Feel its weight and its warmth in your palms.
Can you notice it climbing into your fingers, little by little? [up]
[breathe]
Let your shoulders drop and your back lean against the chair.
There's no hurry: this afternoon is just for you.
You don't have to go anywhere.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The aroma
@energy 1
> Very slow, leaving space between each image.

[soft]Bring the cup close to your face, without drinking yet.
Breathe in its aroma: cocoa, a hint of cinnamon and some vanilla.
[breathe]
The steam rises and warms your nose and cheeks.
Close your eyes and stay with that smell for a moment.
It's an ancient aroma that traveled from the rainforest to your kitchen.
[breathe]
Think of the hands that grew the cacao under the trees.
Of the sun, the rain and the soil that made it grow.
All of that now fits in your cup.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The first sip
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper, savoring each word.

[slow][soft]Now take a small sip, very slowly.
Let the chocolate rest in your mouth before you swallow.
Taste the sweet, the bitter and the creamy, all at once.
[breathe]
Notice how the warmth moves down your chest and stays there.
It's a quiet warmth, like a hug from the inside.
It's a simple pleasure, and it's yours.
[breathe]
If your mind wanders somewhere else, that's okay.
Gently return to the taste in your cup.
This moment doesn't need anything more.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The rain
@energy 2
> Soft and steady, like the rain you describe.

[soft]Listen again to the rain on the roof.
Each drop is a small rhythm, always a little different.
The window looks like a painting in shades of gray.
[breathe]
Breathe with the rain: in, hold, and out.
Let the sound wash away the thoughts of the day.
Whatever worried you can wait outside, like the cold.
[breathe]
In here, everything is warm, slow and **safe**.
When did you last give yourself a pause like this? [up]
Enjoy it without guilt.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Returning
@energy 2
> Come back with calm; a warm tone at the end.

[slow]Finish your cup with one last sip, without hurrying.
Set it down on a plate and rest your warm hands on your legs.[/slow]
[breathe]
Take one more deep breath and notice your whole body.
Move your fingers, stretch your neck a little and open your eyes.
[breathe]
The day is still there, but you return with a different calm.
You can repeat this ritual whenever you need it.
A cup, an aroma and a few minutes just for you. [down]
Thank you for giving yourself this moment.
[pause 3s]
