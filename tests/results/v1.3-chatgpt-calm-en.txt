[breathe]

# Section 2: Along the Mountain Trail
@energy 2
> Keep a steady rhythm, with a gentle sense of movement.

[slow][soft]
Imagine yourself walking along a quiet mountain trail.
Small stones rest beneath your boots, and wild grass moves in the breeze.
[breathe]
You hear the distant sound of water flowing through a valley.
Each step brings you closer to a beautiful lake.
Its surface reflects the clouds, the trees, and the pale morning sky.
[breathe]
You stop beside a smooth rock and look across the water.
A bird glides overhead, following the wind toward the mountains.
There is something **comforting** about this wide, open landscape.
Let the fresh air fill your chest, and release whatever weighs on your mind. [down]
[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Section 3: The Hidden Valley
@energy 1
> Lower your voice and create a feeling of deep stillness.

[slow][soft]
The trail leads you into a sheltered valley.
Here, the wind grows quiet, and the world seems to rest.
[breathe]
A small stream winds between dark stones and patches of green moss.
You notice the soft colors of flowers growing near the water.
Some are white, others purple, and a few shine with golden petals.
[breathe]
Imagine sitting beneath a tree, with your back against its trunk.
You hear the gentle movement of branches above your head.
Nothing needs to change in this **moment**.
You can stay here, breathe, and enjoy the silence. [down]
[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Section 4: Beneath the Evening Sky
@energy 2
> Speak with quiet wonder, allowing each image to settle.

[slow][soft]
As the afternoon passes, the sky begins to change.
Soft orange light spreads across the mountains, touching the snow on their highest peaks.
[breathe]
You find a comfortable place beside the lake.
The water turns silver as the first stars appear overhead.
Look at that beautiful sky!
[breathe]
Far away, a bird calls from the edge of the forest.
You feel the earth beneath you, steady and **safe**.
The evening brings a sense of freedom, as if every worry has drifted away.
What a wonderful place to rest!
[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Section 5: Returning Home
@energy 1
> Slow down toward the end, returning to the present with care.

[slow][soft]
Now imagine the mountains fading into the distance.
The lake, the valley, and the evening sky become a peaceful memory.
[breathe]
You carry that feeling of calm wherever you go.
Begin to notice the surface beneath your body.
Feel the air around you and listen to the sounds nearby.
[breathe]
Take one more deep breath, and let it go.
Move your fingers, relax your jaw, and allow your eyes to open.
You are here, in this **present** moment.
The journey is complete, and you can return whenever you need a little peace. [down]
[/soft][/slow]
