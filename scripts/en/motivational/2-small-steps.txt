# How to start
@energy 4
> Firm and practical.

Choose **one** single habit.
Not five, not ten: **one**.
Make it so simple it's impossible to say no.
If you want to run, start by **putting** on your shoes.
If you want to write, start with a single **sentence**.
Tie it to something you already do every day.
That way you don't depend on memory or motivation.
After coffee, after work, before bed.
[pause 2s]
And **mark** every day you do it.
A cross on the calendar can be powerful.
Watching the chain grow makes you **want** to keep it going.
One rule only: never skip two days in a row.
What if you miss a day? [up]
That's okay: come back the **next** day.
