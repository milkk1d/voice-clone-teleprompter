And we feel **shame**, as if failing said something about who we are.

# How to get back up
@energy 4
> Firm and practical.

When something goes wrong, give **yourself** some time.
Not to punish yourself, but to breathe.
It's okay to feel bad for a while.
Crying, getting angry or walking with no direction is part of the process too.
There's a simple rule: one step at a time.
Then ask yourself three simple **questions**.
What went well, even a little? [up]
What would you do differently next time? [up]
What is the **next** step, however small?
[pause 2s]
Write down the answers.
Putting them on paper makes them **real**.
Then take that step.
Even if it's small, even if nobody sees it.
Don't wait to feel **ready**.
Confidence doesn't come before you try: it comes **after**.
