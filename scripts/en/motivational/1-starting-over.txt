# The first brick

> Firm, with rhythm.

No one builds a house all at once.
Starting over doesn't happen in a day either.
It happens one **brick** at a time.
Choose one single thing you can do **today**.
A phone call, a list or a ten-minute walk.
Small, concrete and **real**.
Do it even if you don't feel like it.
It doesn't have to be perfect, it just has to be done.
Motivation comes **later**, when you see yourself moving forward.
[pause 2s]

Don't look at everything that's missing, just today's brick.
