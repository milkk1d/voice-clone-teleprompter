# The battery
@energy 3
> Calm, warm.

Your phone **warns** you when the battery is low.
You don't have that warning.
Or you do, but you don't **listen** to it.
It's that tiredness a coffee can't fix.
Not one coffee, not two, not three.
That **irritation** over small things.
That feeling that you can't measure up.
That feeling of being everywhere and nowhere at once.
Sound familiar? [up]
[pause 2s]
We live as if our energy were **endless**.
We say yes to everything, sleep little and rest even less.
And then we wonder why we can't **keep** up.
Today I want to talk about something almost nobody teaches you.
Protecting your **energy** is as important as using it.

# Rest is not a prize
@energy 4
> Firm, convinced.

We were **taught** that rest has to be earned.
That first you finish everything, and then, if there's time, you rest.
But the **work** never ends.
And the rest never comes.
[pause 2s]
Rest isn't a **prize**: it's part of the work.
An athlete doesn't train at full power every day.
They rest so their body can **recover** and come back stronger.
Your mind works the same way.
It needs pauses to sort out what it lived through and create something new.
Sleeping well, eating on time and turning off your phone for a while aren't **luxuries**.
They're your **fuel**.
Without fuel, no engine goes very far!

# Boundaries
@energy 4
> Close and brave.

Protecting your energy also means learning to say **no**.
No to the meeting that could have been a message.
No to the commitment you accepted out of guilt.
No to the favor that eats up your whole weekend.
No to the conversation that always takes away your peace.
Saying no is **scary** at first.
We think people will get **upset**.
[pause 2s]
But every time you say no to something that drains you, you say **yes** to something that matters.
To your family, to your health, to your **dreams**.
A boundary isn't a wall against others.
It's a **door** that protects what you love most.
What will you say no to this week? [up]

# Priorities
@energy 4
> Practical and firm.

You can't do **everything**.
And that's okay.
Choose three important things for this week.
Just three.
The ones that move your life where you want it to go.
It's simple.
Everything else can wait, or **someone** else can do it.
A good rule: what matters most comes first.
[pause 2s]
Every morning, ask yourself: what truly matters today? [up]
Start there, while your **energy** is at its highest.
Leave **emails** and small tasks for the afternoon.
And before bed, write down one thing that went **well**.
It seems small, but it changes the way you **end** your day.
And the way you **begin** the next one.

# Your energy is precious
@energy 5
> The close: strong, but warm.

Your energy is the most **precious** thing you have.
More than your time, more than your money.
Because with energy, you can recover almost everything else.
Without energy, time gets **wasted**.
And money can't buy **rest**.
[pause 2s]
So take care of it the way you care for the people you **love**.
Sleep. Breathe. Say no when you need to.
It isn't selfish: it's **responsible**.
The people who love you need your best self, not what's left over.
Will you give yourself that permission today? [up]
I think you **deserve** it.
