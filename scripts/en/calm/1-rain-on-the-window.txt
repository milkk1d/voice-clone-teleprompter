# Arriving
@energy 2
> Speak slowly, like walking someone to sleep.

[slow][soft]Find a comfortable position, in bed or in an armchair.
Let your body rest fully on whatever holds it.
Close your eyes whenever you like.
Notice your eyelids growing heavy.
[breathe]
Outside, it starts to rain.
Listen to the first drops tapping on the glass, one by one.
[breathe]
You don't have to go anywhere now.
You're indoors, with a warm blanket over your legs.
Can you hear the rain becoming steadier? [up][/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The sound
@energy 2
> Soft, unhurried.

[soft]The rain falls on the roof, on the trees and on the empty street.
It's a constant sound that asks for nothing.
[breathe]
Let your breathing follow its rhythm.
Breathe in as the drops fall.
Breathe out as the water runs down the window.
Breathe in again, without hurry, and breathe out with ease.
[breathe]
Each drop that slides down the glass carries a thought away.
You don't have to stop them: just watch them go.
One, and another, and another.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The room
@energy 1
> Very soft. Like a whisper.

[slow][soft]Imagine the room in dim light, warm and quiet.
Maybe it smells of tea, of wood or of clean sheets.
[breathe]
Feel the weight of the soft blanket on your legs.
Your feet loosen, your ankles let go.
Your knees rest.
Your hands open on the blanket, holding nothing.
[breathe]
Outside, the storm passes by.
Inside, everything is calm.
Here, you are **safe**.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Letting go
@energy 1
> Slow, almost a whisper.

[slow][soft]Now the rain grows finer, almost a murmur.
Let your shoulders drop a little more.
Relax your forehead, your jaw and your tongue.
[breathe]
If a worry comes, imagine writing it on the fogged-up window.
Watch the drops wash it away, little by little.
One by one, your worries turn into water.
[breathe]
Do you notice your body growing heavier? [up]
There's nothing to fix tonight, and nothing to plan for tomorrow.
Just listen, and enjoy the simple pleasure of the rain.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Coming back
@energy 2
> Gently, coming back to the present.

[soft]Little by little, the rain stops.
Only a few drops are left, falling from the roof.
The air smells of wet earth, clean and fresh.
[breathe]
Bring your attention to your breath, now slow and deep.
If you're going to sleep, stay here, in this calm.
If you're going on with your day, move your fingers and toes.
[breathe]
Stretch your arms gently.
Open your eyes whenever you like.
Take a little of this silence with you.[/soft]
[pause 3s]
