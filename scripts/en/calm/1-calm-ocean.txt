# Arriving
@energy 2
> Speak slowly, as if the listener were already half asleep.

[slow][soft]Find a comfortable position and let your body grow heavy.
Let the chair or the bed hold all of your weight.
Close your eyes whenever you're ready.
[breathe]
Breathe in through your nose, deep and unhurried.
Exhale through your mouth, like a wave pulling back from the shore.
[breathe]
Notice your shoulders, your jaw and your hands.
Let each one soften a little more.
Can you feel your body starting to slow down? [up]
There's nothing to do but be here.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The surface
@energy 2
> Paint the scene gently. No rush.

[soft]Now imagine you're floating on a calm sea.
The water is warm, and the sky is pale blue with a few white clouds.
Small waves rise and fall beneath your back, like a slow breath.
[breathe]
Can you feel the sun resting on your face? [up]
Far away, a seagull calls, and then everything is silent again.
There's nothing to fix and nowhere to be.
[breathe]
Just the gentle rocking of the water, again and again.
Each wave carries away a little of the day's **tiredness**.
And you keep floating, calm and at ease.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Sinking down
@energy 1
> Even slower now. Let the words sink with the listener.

[slow][soft]Now let yourself sink, very gently, below the surface.
The light turns green, then deep blue, then softer still.
[breathe]
With every breath, you drift a little deeper.
Ten meters, twenty meters… the noise of the world stays above.
Down here you can breathe with ease, like in a dream.
[breathe]
Far ahead, a whale passes by, singing a long, low song.
Its voice fills the water and reaches your chest.
Listen without thinking about anything.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The quiet deep
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper, but every word clear.

[soft]Here, at the bottom of the ocean, everything is still.
Tiny lights glow around you, like stars in a dark sky.
A glass-like jellyfish drifts past, shining violet.
[breathe]
You are safe and calm; the water holds you.
Over the sand and the stones, everything rests.
If a thought comes, let it rise like a bubble.
Watch it float toward the surface, without following it.
[breathe]
What remains when the thoughts are gone? [up]
Only your breathing, slow and deep.
Stay here a moment longer.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Returning
@energy 2
> Gently bring the listener back. A warmer tone at the end.

[slow]Now, little by little, begin to rise toward the light.
Feel the water grow warmer and clearer around you.[/slow]
[breathe]
Take one deep breath and let it fill your whole chest.
Wiggle your fingers and your toes.
Notice the surface beneath your body and the air in the room.
[breathe]
When you're ready, open your eyes.
Carry this calm with you, like a small treasure from the deep.
Come back to it whenever you need it. [down]
[pause 3s]
