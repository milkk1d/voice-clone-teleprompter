# Arriving
@energy 2
> A soft, unhurried voice.

[slow][soft]Find a comfortable position and rest your back well.
Let your hands open, palms facing up.
Close your eyes.
[breathe]
Breathe in through your nose and feel the cool air.
Breathe out through your mouth, without hurry.
Once more, as if the air came down from the mountain.
[breathe]
You don't have to go anywhere now.
Can you notice the weight of your body on the seat? [up][/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The riverbank
@energy 2
> Paint the landscape with calm.

[soft]Imagine sitting on the bank of a mountain river.
The water is crystal clear, almost silver, flowing between round stones.
[breathe]
Around you there are tall trees, and the air smells of pine.
A bird sings on a branch somewhere, then falls quiet.
Higher up, a glacier still holds a little white snow.
[breathe]
Listen to the sound of the river, steady and fresh.
It never stops, but it never rushes either.
It just keeps going.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The water
@energy 1
> Very soft.

[slow][soft]Watch how the water flows around each stone without fighting it.
It touches it, goes around it and moves on.
[breathe]
Now imagine your worries are leaves.
Pick them up one by one and set them on the water.
One leaf for each thing that weighs on you today.
[breathe]
Watch the river carry them away, downstream.
You don't need to know where they're going.
The river knows the way.
Just watch them **drift** away.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Cool and calm
@energy 1
> Slow, almost a whisper.

[slow][soft]Imagine dipping your hands into the water.
It's cold and clean, and it gently wakes up your fingers.
[breathe]
That coolness rises up your arms and clears your mind.
Your head feels lighter.
The morning sun warms your back.
[breathe]
How long has it been since you listened only to water? [up]
Stay here a moment longer, a quiet treasure of time.
Let the sound of the water fill all the space around you.
No thinking, no doing, just listening.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Coming back
@energy 2
> Gently, coming back to the present.

[soft]Little by little, the sound of the river grows distant.
Take with you that feeling of clear water and fresh air.
Keep it in your chest, like a small treasure.
[breathe]
Notice your breathing again, and the place where you are.
Move your fingers and toes.
Stretch your back with care.
[breathe]
Open your eyes whenever you like.
The river is still flowing, even if you can't see it, and you can always come back.[/soft]
[pause 3s]
