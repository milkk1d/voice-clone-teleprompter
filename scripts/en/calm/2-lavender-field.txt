# Arriving
@energy 2
> A warm, slow voice, like a summer afternoon.

[slow][soft]Sit or lie down in a comfortable position.
Rest your hands on your legs or by your sides.
Close your eyes, without hurry.
[breathe]
Breathe in through your nose, as if you were smelling a flower.
Let it out through your mouth, taking your time.
Once more, feeling your chest fill and empty.
[breathe]
There's nothing left to do but be here.
Can you feel the air moving in and out on its own? [up][/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The path
@energy 2
> Paint the scene with calm.

[soft]Imagine you're walking along a dirt path in the afternoon.
All around you grows a field of lavender, purple to the horizon.
[breathe]
The afternoon sun warms your back.
A warm breeze moves the flowers, which bend and rise again.
[breathe]
Every step is slow.
Your feet sink a little into the soft, warm earth.
No clock, no rules, no hurry.
Just the path, the sun and the scent of the flowers.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The scent
@energy 1
> Very soft.

[slow][soft]You stop and take a deep breath.
The lavender smells soft, clean and a little sweet.
[breathe]
With each breath in, that scent drops down to your chest.
With each breath out, it carries away some of the day's **tiredness**.
[breathe]
Listen to the bees moving from flower to flower, and to a small bird somewhere.
Their hum is steady, like a lullaby.
Your shoulders loosen.
Your breathing grows deep and even.
Your forehead softens.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Resting
@energy 1
> Slow, almost a whisper.

[slow][soft]Imagine lying down among the flowers, on the warm earth.
The sky is pale blue, with a few white clouds drifting by.
[breathe]
Feel the ground holding you completely.
Your legs are heavy, your arms are heavy.
Even your thoughts seem to move slower, like the clouds.
[breathe]
If a thought appears, let it float away like a cloud.
Can you see it drifting off, pushed by the wind? [up]
You don't need to follow it.
Just watch the sky and breathe.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Coming back
@energy 2
> Gently, coming back to the present.

[soft]Little by little, the sun begins to set, and the field turns golden.
You get up with calm and walk back along the same path.
[breathe]
Take with you the scent of lavender and the warmth of the afternoon.
Keep this moment like a small treasure.
Now, notice again the place where you are.
Move your fingers and gently stretch your neck.
[breathe]
Open your eyes whenever you like.
That field is still there, and you can come back whenever you need it.[/soft]
[pause 3s]
