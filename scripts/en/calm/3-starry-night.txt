# Arriving
@energy 2
> A very soft voice, at night.

[slow][soft]Find a comfortable position and let your body be still.
If you can, turn off the lights around you.
Close your eyes.
[breathe]
Breathe in slowly, counting to four.
Breathe out, counting to six.
Again: in for four, out for six.
[breathe]
Once more: the air comes in, the air goes out.
There's no hurry anymore.
Do you notice your body starting to feel heavy? [up][/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The field at night
@energy 2
> Paint the scene with calm.

[soft]Imagine you're in an open field, far from the city.
It's a warm night, with no wind.
[breathe]
You lie back on the grass, with a blanket under your back.
In the distance, crickets sing, soft and steady.
It smells of earth and fresh grass.
The night wraps around you like a warm hug.
[breathe]
Above you, the sky is completely dark.
And little by little, your eyes begin to see the first stars.
One, two, ten, a hundred.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The stars
@energy 1
> Very slow.

[slow][soft]Now the sky is full of tiny lights.
Some shine bright; others are faint.
That light traveled for centuries to reach you, a quiet treasure.
[breathe]
Look at that pale band crossing the sky, like a river of white dust.
It's our galaxy, seen from the inside.
[breathe]
Under such a vast sky, your worries become small.
They don't disappear, but they stop weighing so much.
You watch them from far away, with the same calm you feel watching the sky.
Breathe, and let the sky keep you **company**.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Letting go of the day
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper.

[slow][soft]Think of something that happened today, something small.
A conversation, a noise, a task.
[breathe]
Imagine placing it on one of those faraway stars.
It can stay there tonight.
The star will keep it for you until tomorrow.
[breathe]
Is there anything else you'd like to leave up there? [up]
Let it go gently, without judging.
It doesn't matter if it was big or small.
One star for each thing from your day.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# Coming back
@energy 2
> Gently, coming back to the present.

[soft]Little by little, feel again the surface that holds you.
The bed, the couch or the floor beneath your body.
Notice the weight of your arms and the warmth of your hands.
[breathe]
If you're going to sleep, keep this image of the sky and let it carry you.
If you're going on with your night, gently move your hands.
[breathe]
Stretch your body with calm.
Open your eyes whenever you like.
The stars are still there, even when you can't see them.[/soft]
[pause 3s]
