# Arriving at the forest
@energy 2
> Slow and kind, like opening a door.

[slow][soft]Close your eyes and let your breathing find its own pace.
Imagine you're at the edge of a southern forest, in Patagonia.
[breathe]
The air is fresh and smells of damp earth and wood.
The trees are tall, with ancient trunks covered in moss.
Can you hear the leaves crunch beneath your steps? [up]
[breathe]
Walk slowly, with no hurry at all.
Each step takes you further from the noise of the city.
You're here now, and that's enough.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The trail
@energy 1
> Very soft; leave space between each image.

[soft]The trail winds between ferns, plants and roots.
Light comes through the branches in thin rays.
[breathe]
A small bird sings far away, and another one answers.
Feel the soft ground under your feet, firm and steady.
With every step, your shoulders feel a little lighter.
[breathe]
If a thought appears, let it go like a leaf in the wind.
You don't have to follow it.
Just walk, breathe and look around.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# The lake
@energy 1
> Almost a whisper, with deep calm.

[slow][soft]All at once, the forest opens and a lake of clear water appears.
It's so still that it mirrors the mountains like glass.
[breathe]
You sit on a warm stone by the shore.
Look at the water: it doesn't move, it isn't in a hurry.
Your mind can be like that too.
[breathe]
Breathe with the lake: slow on the way in, slower on the way out.
Listen to the silence, which here is vast and soft.
Stay for a moment without doing anything.[/soft][/slow]
[pause 3s]

# The snow
@energy 2
> Soft, with a touch of wonder.

[soft]High above, on the peaks, the **snow** shines in the afternoon sun.
Imagine that clean white entering your chest with every breath.
[breathe]
As you exhale, let tiredness leave, like a gray cloud drifting away.
Breathe in white, breathe out gray, again and again.
Your body feels lighter with each round.
[breathe]
Do you notice how much space there is inside you now? [up]
That space is yours too.
You can come back to it whenever you want.[/soft]
[pause 3s]

# Returning
@energy 2
> Come back with care; a warm tone at the end.

[slow]Say goodbye to the lake with one last look.
Walk back along the trail, unhurried, between the trees.[/slow]
[breathe]
Little by little, the forest stays behind and you return to your room.
Feel the weight of your body and the air around you.
[breathe]
Move your hands, stretch your back and open your eyes when you're ready.
Take the forest's silence with you.
It's kept inside you, a quiet treasure for whenever you need it. [down]
Thank you for this walk.
[pause 3s]
