# Three tricks that worked

> Explain with clarity, no lecturing.

Since that day, I've tried lots of things, and I'll share the three that **worked** for me.
The first is the five-minute rule.
I tell myself: "I'll only work for five minutes, and then I can stop."
I almost never stop, because the hard part was starting, not continuing.
The second is breaking tasks into ridiculously small steps.
I don't write "finish the report," but "open the document" or "write the title."
The third is taking my phone out of the room while I work.
