# My tips
@energy 3
> Practical tone, like advice between friends.

If you're going to a big city for the first time, here are three tips.
First, download the map to your phone, in case you lose signal.
Second, walk without rushing, because the **best** places aren't in the guidebooks.
Third, and this is the most important one: ask.
[pause]
Locals know things no app will ever tell you.
I mean, the best map is a **person** who lives there.
Always carry your address written on a piece of paper.
And if you get lost, it's no big deal.
Sometimes getting lost is the best way to get to know a place.
That's how I found my favorite café, to be honest.
And a few friends too.
