# What Wi-Fi is
@energy 3
> Explain it simply, like to a friend.

First things first: Wi-Fi is **not** the same as the internet.
Wi-Fi is just the path between your phone and the box at home, the famous router.
That box gets internet through a cable and spreads it through the air.
It's a bit like a waiter carrying plates from the kitchen to your table.
It does this with radio waves, similar to the ones in a car radio.
Look, they're invisible, but they fill your whole home.
[breathe]
So if the internet is down, your Wi-Fi can look perfect and still load nothing.
I mean, sometimes the problem isn't your Wi-Fi, it's your **provider**.
Knowing that saved me a lot of useless calls.
And plenty of frustration, let me tell you.

# Wi-Fi's enemies
@energy 4
> Energy rises here: this is the most useful part.

Now for the good part: the things that **block** your signal.
Thick brick walls, mirrors and metal furniture are its worst enemies.
Water also absorbs the signal, so a fish tank or lots of big plants can get in the way.
And the microwave!
When it's running, it can interfere with some home networks.
[pause]
I had my router hidden behind the television, inside a cabinet.
What a mistake!
I moved it to a **high**, open spot, facing the hallway, in the middle of the house.
The difference was huge.
Even in the kitchen, which used to be a dead zone, the signal now reaches.

# Little tricks
@energy 3
> Practical tone, like advice between friends.

Here are some other tricks that worked for me.
Restart the router every now and then: very often that **fixes** everything.
Change the password it came with from the factory, for your security.
And check how many devices are connected.
[breathe]
Once I found a device I didn't recognize.
It turned out to be an old printer, connected for years.
Can you believe it? [up]
If you have guests, create a separate network just for them.
If your home is big, there are **repeaters** that carry the signal farther.
They're not expensive and they can rescue that room where nothing ever reaches.
With those changes, my Wi-Fi went from drama to working without problems.
