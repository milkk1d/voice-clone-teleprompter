# The detour
@energy 2
> A low voice, a night drive.

Sofia was driving at night on a southern road, with an almost empty tank.
Her phone's navigation app had lost signal an hour earlier.
Then she saw a wooden sign, half hidden by fog: "Valle Quieto, two kilometers."
She didn't remember that town on any map.
But she needed **gas** and a place to sleep.
She crossed a bridge and took the detour.
The dirt road wound down between tall, silent trees.
[pause 2s]
All at once, the **first** lights appeared.
A small town, with stone houses, a square and a church with a clock.
Everything seemed calm, too calm.
Why was there no one in the streets, if it was only nine o'clock? [up]

# The town
@energy 2
> Slow, with detail.

[slow][soft]Sofia parked in front of an inn with lit windows.
Inside, an old woman was knitting by the fireplace.
"Welcome to Valle Quieto," she said without looking up. "We've been expecting you."
Sofia smiled, a little puzzled.[/soft][/slow]
She thought it was just a friendly way of talking.
The woman gave her a brass key and a bowl of hot soup.
The room had an iron bed, an oval mirror and a calendar on the wall.
The calendar showed a very old **year**.
She didn't think it was unusual.
She was exhausted after so many hours on the road.
She fell asleep listening to the church clock, which struck the hours with a **deep** sound.

# The morning

> Very slow. Something doesn't fit.

The next morning, the town was full of people.
Children played in the square, a baker pulled bread from the oven and a dog slept in the sun.
Everyone greeted her by name, though she hadn't told it to anyone.
[soft]Nobody had a phone. Nobody had a car.[/soft]
The shop signs were painted by hand, with letters from another time.
Sofia asked where she could find a gas station.
"You won't need one here," the baker said with a smile.
"Whoever comes to Valle Quieto never needs to leave."
A shiver ran down Sofia's spine.
[pause 2s]
She went back to the inn for her things, but her car was no longer in the **street**.
In its place was a wooden cart, with a white **horse**.

# The reveal
@energy 4
> The reveal: firm, no shouting.

Sofia ran to the church and climbed the bell tower.
From the top, she could see the whole valley.
[pause 2s]
And then she understood.
The town had no roads **out**.
The streets ended in the fog, as if someone had erased them.
On the wall of the bell tower was a brass plaque with a very old date.
"Here stood Valle Quieto, until the river covered it."
[slow][soft]The town had been underwater for more than a hundred years.[/soft][/slow]
But from that tower, the river was nowhere to be seen.
Where, then, was the **water**? [up]
And where was she? [up]
