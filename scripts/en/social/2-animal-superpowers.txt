# The bat
@energy 5
> The closing clip: high energy.

Can you "see" with your **ears**? [up]
A bat can.
It makes high-pitched sounds you can't **hear**.
The sound **bounces** off things and returns as an echo.
[fast]That's how it measures distance, size and even texture.[/fast]
It's called echolocation.
It's like a natural radar.
With it, it catches **insects** in total darkness.
Some eat hundreds of insects in a single night.
They're free pest **control**!
They also help pollinate many flowers.
Many tropical **fruits** depend on them.
