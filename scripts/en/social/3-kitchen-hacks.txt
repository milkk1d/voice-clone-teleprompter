# Onions without tears
@energy 5
> Knowing and friendly.

Nobody tells you this trick for cutting **onions**.
Before cutting, chill it in the **fridge** a few minutes.
Cold slows down the gases that make you cry.
Those gases are released when you break its cells.
Always use a **sharp** knife.
Why? [up]
Because a dull knife **crushes** the onion.
And crushing it releases more of those gases.
Cut fast, with firm movements.
[fast]Cold onion, sharp knife and quick cuts.[/fast]
Goodbye, **tears**!
Well, **almost** all of them.

# Stale bread
@energy 5
> A quick, cheerful pace.

Is your bread rock **hard**? [up]
Don't **throw** it out.
Pass it **fast** under running water.
Just a few **seconds**, without soaking it.
Then put it in a hot **oven** for five minutes.
[fast]The water turns to steam and softens the crumb.[/fast]
It comes out crunchy outside and soft **inside**!
Like it's fresh from the bakery.
Your family won't notice the difference.
It works with baguettes, rolls and homemade loaves.
But eat it soon: it hardens again within **hours**.

# Fresh herbs
@energy 5
> Practical and full of energy.

Does your cilantro wilt in two **days**? [up]
Treat it like a **bouquet** of flowers.
Trim the ends of the stems.
Put it in a glass with a little water.
Cover it with a bag and keep it **cold**.
[fast]It works with parsley, cilantro and mint.[/fast]
It stays fresh for up to a **week**!
Change the water every two days.
And remove any leaves that turn yellow.
Basil is different: it prefers **room** temperature.
The cold turns its leaves dark.
Your grandma probably knew this already.
Save this trick and stop throwing away **food**.

# The fresh egg test
@energy 5
> Like a quick experiment.

Is this egg still **fresh**? [up]
There's a very easy test.
Fill a glass with cold **water**.
Gently place the egg inside.
Watch what it does at the bottom.
[fast]If it sinks and lies flat, it's fresh.[/fast]
If it stands up on the bottom, use it **soon**.
If it floats, better not **eat** it.
Why does this happen? [up]
Over time, **air** gets in through the shell.
And the air **pocket** inside the egg grows.
A glass of water saves you a nasty surprise!
Share this with whoever cooks at your house.

# Fluffy rice
@energy 5
> Confident, like sharing a secret.

Does your rice always come out **sticky**? [up]
The problem isn't the pot.
It's not the brand of rice either.
It's the **starch**.
The golden rule: rinse before you cook.
Swirl it with your **hand** and change the water often.
Until the water runs almost clear.
Then drain it well before cooking.
[fast]Rinse, drain, toast a little and cook.[/fast]
After cooking, let it **rest**, covered, for five minutes.
And fluff it with a **fork**, not a spoon!
Separate grains, like at a **restaurant**.

# Avocado and lemon
@energy 5
> The closing clip: high energy.

Two quick tricks with **fruit**.
First, the avocado.
Once it's cut, it turns **brown** in minutes.
Squeeze a few drops of lemon on top.
Just a little, no need to overdo it.
[fast]The acid slows the browning and protects it.[/fast]
Store it with the **pit** in and well covered.
Second, the lemon.
Want to get more **juice** out of it? [up]
Before cutting, roll it on the counter.
Press down with your palm as you roll.
The cells inside break and release more **liquid**.
More juice from the same **lemon**!
It's a small pleasure in the kitchen.
