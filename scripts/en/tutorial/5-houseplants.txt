# The basics
@energy 3
> A warm, patient tone.

Have your plants ever dried up even though you cared for them with love? [up]
Don't worry, it has happened to almost everyone.
Today I'll explain the three secrets to keep your houseplants **thriving**.
They are light, water and the most common mistakes.
The good **news** is that most houseplants are very tough.
They just need you to understand what they're asking for, and it isn't much.
And believe me, plants do talk, even without words.
Caring for them is a quiet pleasure.
Let's start with the first secret: **light**.

# Step one: light
@energy 3
> Clear and concrete.

First, notice how much **light** each corner of your home gets.
Most houseplants prefer bright but indirect light.
That means near a window, but without direct sun on the leaves.
If the leaves burn or turn yellow, they may be getting **too** much sun.
If the stems grow long and lean toward the window, they're looking for more light.
Plants like pothos or snake plants handle darker corners well.
If your home is very dark, a grow light can help.
[loud]Careful with sudden changes: moving a plant every day stresses it.[/loud]
Choose a good spot and leave it there, unless it shows you it isn't **happy**.
[pause]

# Step two: water
@energy 3
> Patient, step by step.

Second, learn one rule: water according to the **plant**, not the calendar.
The most common mistake isn't forgetting to water, but watering too **much**.
Before watering, push a finger into the soil up to the second knuckle.
If it's damp, wait; if it's dry, it's time to water.
Water until it drains out of the holes at the bottom, then empty the saucer.
Succulents and cacti need water only once in a while.
And in winter? [up]
In winter, almost all plants **drink** less, so space out your watering.
[pause]

# Step three: common mistakes
@energy 3
> Firm and kind.

Third, get to know the mistakes that kill the most plants.
One is using pots without holes: the water sits there and rots the **roots**.
Another is placing plants next to the heater, the air conditioner or a cold window.
It's also common to never clean the leaves.
Dust blocks light, so wipe them with a damp **cloth** every few weeks.
[loud]Careful: some houseplants are toxic to cats and dogs.[/loud]
If you have pets, check each species before bringing it home.
A healthy plant starts with a **safe** place!
[pause]

# Summary
@energy 3
> A cheerful close.

Let's review the three secrets.
First, **bright** but indirect light, in a fixed spot.
Second, water when the soil is dry, not out of **habit**.
Third, pots with holes, clean leaves and care around pets.
If a plant looks sad, don't give up: you can almost always bring it back.
With a little attention, your home will fill with **life**!
