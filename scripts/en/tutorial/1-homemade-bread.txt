# What you'll need
@energy 3
> A patient teacher's tone, clear and friendly.

Today you'll learn to make homemade bread, with no machines and just a few ingredients.
I'll walk you through it step by step, the way people have done it for centuries.
Did you know that flour, water, salt and yeast are all you need? [up]
For a medium loaf, you need five hundred grams of **flour**.
Plus three hundred and twenty-five milliliters of warm water, ten grams of salt and seven of dry yeast.
Have a large bowl, a wooden spoon and a clean towel ready.
We'll use the oven at the **end**, so don't turn it on yet.
The secret of good bread isn't strength, it's **patience**.
Shall we start? [up]

# Step one: mix
@energy 3
> Clear and patient.

First, **dissolve** the yeast in the warm water and wait five minutes.
You'll see a soft foam form on the surface.
That means the yeast is **alive** and ready to work.
If no foam appears, the yeast is old and it's best to replace it.
In the bowl, **mix** the flour with the salt.
Make a well in the center and pour in the water with the yeast.
Stir with the spoon until no dry flour is left.
[loud]Careful: the water shouldn't be hot, because heat kills the yeast.[/loud]
It should feel warm on your wrist, like a baby's bath.
[pause]

# Step two: knead
@energy 3
> A steady rhythm, with calm energy.

Second, move the dough to a table dusted with flour and **knead** it.
Push with your palm, fold the dough over itself and turn it.
Repeat that movement for about **ten** minutes.
At first the dough will be sticky, but don't add too much flour.
Little by little, it will become smooth, stretchy and **soft**.
How do you know it's ready? [up]
Stretch it with care: if it forms a thin sheet without tearing, it's done.
That's the most satisfying moment of the whole process!
[pause]

# Step three: rise and bake
@energy 3
> Explain with calm.

Third, leave the dough in the bowl, covered with the towel or with plastic, to **rise**.
It needs one to two hours in a warm place, away from drafts.
You'll know it's ready when it has **doubled** in size.
If you press it with a finger, the dent should spring back slowly.
Then shape it into a ball and put it on a tray with baking paper.
Make a cut on top with a sharp knife.
To finish, bake it at two hundred and twenty degrees for about **thirty** minutes.
[loud]Careful when you open the oven: the hot steam burns.[/loud]
The bread is ready when the crust is crunchy and it sounds hollow underneath.
[pause]

# Summary
@energy 3
> A friendly close, like reviewing with the group.

Let's review what we did today.
First, we dissolved the yeast and mixed the ingredients.
Second, we kneaded until the dough was smooth and **stretchy**.
Third, we let it rise, shaped it and baked it.
Before slicing it, let the bread **cool** for at least twenty minutes.
That way, the inside finishes cooking.
Keep it wrapped in a towel, never in the fridge.
It's a small pleasure that fills the whole house with a wonderful smell.
And now, enjoy your first **homemade** loaf!
With practice, you can add seeds, herbs or cheese.
