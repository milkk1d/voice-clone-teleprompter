# The station
@energy 2
> Narrate tenderly.

At the little station of San Lorenzo, the seven o'clock train always arrived on time.
And always, on the platform, a dog the color of **cinnamon**, with one floppy ear, was waiting for it.
No one knew where he had come from.
Rafael, the stationmaster, called him Captain, because he seemed to inspect every car.
He saved him bread, a little rice and fresh water in an old dish.
Captain never got on the train.
He only wagged his tail when the doors opened, as if he were greeting each passenger.
The travelers got used to him.
Some brought him cookies, which he ate with pleasure; others patted his head before boarding.
"Who are you waiting for, my friend?" Rafael would ask him every morning.
The dog just looked at him with calm eyes, as if the answer were **obvious**.

# The day of the fair
@energy 3
> The tension builds.

One spring Saturday, the train arrived full of families heading to the town fair.
There were balloons, music and a noise Captain had never heard before.
In the crowd, a five-year-old girl let go of her mother's hand to look at a **red** balloon.
When she turned around, her mother was gone.
[pause 2s]
Lucia started walking between legs and suitcases, farther and farther away.
She reached the end of the platform, where the roof ended and the tracks began.
She sat down on the edge and started to cry.
On the other side of the station, her mother was calling her name again and again.
"Lucia! Lucia!"
The stationmaster dropped everything to look for her, but there were too many **people**.

# Captain
@energy 4
> The climax: urgency and relief.

Then Captain lifted his floppy ear and broke into a run.
He shot through the crowd like an arrow, dodging bags, wheels and shoes.
He found Lucia at the edge of the platform, just as a freight train was approaching.
He stood in front of her and barked once, **loud**.
The little girl jumped and stepped back toward the wall.
[pause 2s]
The train roared past a few meters away.
Captain lay down beside her and rested his head on her knees.
Lucia stopped crying and hugged him.
That was how Rafael and her mother found them, a minute later.
"This dog is a **hero**!" the woman said, her voice breaking.
Rafael smiled: "I always knew he was waiting for something."

# Back home
@energy 2
> Calm. End with a smile.

Lucia's family wanted to take Captain home with them.
Rafael agreed, although that night he missed his friend on the platform.
But the next morning, at seven o'clock sharp, something **curious** happened.
Down the road from town came Lucia, with her school backpack, and Captain, wagging his tail.
The dog sat on the platform, as always, to wait for the train.
[soft]When the doors opened, he greeted the passengers one by one.[/soft]
Then he walked Lucia to school, crossing the tracks at the pedestrian crossing.
Since that day, Captain has two jobs and two homes.
In the evenings, he sleeps beside Lucia's bed, with his floppy ear on the pillow.
And Rafael no longer wonders who he's **waiting** for. [down]
