# The trail
@energy 2
> A calm voice, like remembering a trip.

Ana and her brother Tomas arrived in Patagonia at the end of February.
They wanted to hike to a glacier that only came into view after six hours on the trail.
The park ranger watched them from the window of his wooden cabin.
"Check the forecast. Out here, the wind changes in minutes," he warned them.
Tomas laughed and tightened the straps of his backpack.
"We've got water, food and a map. What could go wrong?" [up]
The morning was bright and the sky a **clean**, deep blue.
They walked among twisted trees, icy rivers and moss-covered stones.
In the distance, a condor drew wide circles above the hills.
Ana stopped every few **meters** to take photos, just for the pleasure of it.
For the first time in months, she felt she could breathe without hurry.

# The storm
@energy 3
> The tension rises all at once.

In the middle of the afternoon, the clouds dropped down from the mountains.
The wind began to blow with a **force** Ana had never felt.
The gusts kept pushing them toward the edge of the trail.
"Get down!" Tomas shouted, grabbing her arm.
They threw themselves to the ground behind a large rock, hearts racing.
The rain came in sideways, cold and hard, like tiny stones.
[pause 2s]
Tomas tried to stand and fell with a **groan**.
He had twisted his ankle.
Ana looked at the soaked map, then at the sky, which was growing darker.
The nearest shelter was an hour away.
With his ankle like that, it would take at least **three**.

# The shelter
@energy 4
> The climax: firmness and relief.

"Lean on me and don't look down," Ana said, with a calm she didn't feel.
They moved forward step by step, counting under their breath so they wouldn't think about the cold.
Every gust forced them to stop and wait.
Ana gathered what little energy she had left and kept going.
[pause 2s]
Through the fog, all of a sudden, she saw a **yellow** light.
"There it is, Tomas, the shelter!" she cried, almost out of voice.
The door opened before they reached it.
It was the park ranger, with a flashlight in one hand and a blanket over his shoulder.
"I've been looking for you for **two** hours," he said, helping them inside.
In the cabin, a wood stove crackled next to a pot of hot tea.
Ana dropped onto a bench and, at last, she **cried**.

# The glacier
@energy 2
> Calm after the storm. End with a smile.

The next morning, the wind was gone as if it had never existed.
The ranger wrapped Tomas's ankle and served bowls of warm oatmeal.
"You don't conquer Patagonia. You respect it," he told them as he passed the bread.
Ana stepped out of the shelter with her mug in both hands.
[soft]In front of her, the glacier shone with a blue that seemed to come from another world.[/soft]
It was the place they had come to find.
It was **right** there, a few kilometers away, but neither of them wanted to keep walking.
Tomas limped to the door and asked: "Shall we come back next summer?" [up]
Ana smiled and handed him the mug.
"We'll come back, but this time we'll check the **forecast**."
They both laughed, watching the ice in **silence**. [down]
